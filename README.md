**Solmar Heat** — an open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Explore the sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar on foot and by car: steal parked and traffic cars, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs, take jobs for the fixer Mara, and pay off your debt to Sal across 3 story missions. Traffic and pedestrians fill the streets, combat is fists and a pistol, and the HUD shows health, money, wanted stars and a live minimap. Progress saves to your browser so you can continue later.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Keyboard + mouse: WASD to move/drive, E to enter or exit a car, F or Space to punch, click to shoot, Shift to run, P or Esc to pause, M to mute.

## Hand it in

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
